Our project is about capture: something that has to find its target and hold on to it before the moment passes. This is that idea turned into a minute of play, and nothing more — it is here for outreach and for fun, and it is not a model of our system or a representation of any result.

You have sixty seconds. Logos arrive in the dish on their own schedule — a new one does not wait for the last one to go, so there are usually two or three out at once, and sometimes a beat with none. Each one waits, and each one goes. Catch them with the net. How long one waits before it goes is up to you.
