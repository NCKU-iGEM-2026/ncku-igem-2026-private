Gold Medal

Gold asks for every Bronze and Silver requirement, and then for excellence in the three Special Awards a team names on its Judging Form. Ours were Best New Composite Part, Best Education and Best Hardware.

Best Infectious Diseases Project

The award for a project in infectious diseases. Capture is aimed at the signals Pseudomonas aeruginosa uses to coordinate a hospital infection: one system detects the signal and reports the infection, the other inhibits it, so the coordination it carries does not take place.
