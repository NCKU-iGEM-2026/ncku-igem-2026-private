Overview

From Lab to Society

As scientists, we can lose sight of the world outside the lab. An innovation that succeeds at the bench is not automatically one that works in the real world, and the knowledge and spirit of the lab do not always reach the society around it. So one question stayed with us all year: how can our technology actually serve society, and how can more people come to understand what we are doing?

That is why this year NCKU-Tainan put Integrated Human Practices (IHP) at the centre of our work, and took it in four directions. Choose one to follow it.

Integrated Human Practices

What We Heard, What We Did

Going out was only half of it. Each direction brought something back, and what came back changed what we did next.

  1. ↑ Rising Dr. Xin Min Liao Apr 10
    We heard

    Only about 20% of antibiotic treatment is guided by susceptibility results, and identifying the bacterium takes days. Screening before symptoms is a long shot; a fast way to identify the species — even Pseudomonas alone — would have real potential.

    We did

    We aimed Capture at that need: telling quickly whether P. aeruginosa is there, rather than screening before symptoms.

    →
  2. → Reaching NCKU Pilot Interdisciplinary Sharing Session May 23
    We heard

    Panelists from fields far from synthetic biology questioned assumptions we had stopped questioning ourselves.

    We did

    We narrowed Capture from a broad entry point around ventilator-associated pneumonia to the infection mechanisms of P. aeruginosa.

    →
  3. ↑ Rising Prof. Shu-Chu Shiesh May 27
    We heard

    Aptamer detection has already been built into a urinary-catheter chip. An aptamer works only when folded correctly, which depends on buffer pH and specific ions — so test buffers in different proportions.

    We did

    Encouraged to try it, we went ahead: the following week we ordered the synthetic aptamer (ssDNA) for our inhibition test.

    →
  4. ← Returning 2026 Capture survey Jul 6 · 376 responses
    We heard

    Only 37.0% had heard of P. aeruginosa infections, and many were neutral or hesitant about gene-edited bacterial drugs.

    We did

    It drove our later workshops and outreach: explaining the infection, and demystifying genetic engineering.

    →
  5. ↓ Rooting Science camps with STEAM from Seed Jul 13 – Aug 21
    We heard

    The same lessons landed differently with children of different ages and levels.

    We did

    We learned to make complex science concrete enough for each of them, through trial and error from one camp to the next.

    →
  6. ↑ Rising Ai Wei Li Laser Engraving Studio Sep 10
    We heard

    Every material reflects light differently, and a joint of the wrong thickness lets light leak in.

    We did

    We took their advice on materials and fabrication, and assembled and adjusted the hardware at the studio with them.

    →
  7. ↑ Rising Prof. Tsung Lin Liao Sep 22
    We heard

    Decide what matters most in the hardware, check how enzymes in patient samples might degrade our DNA sequences, and try a wider range of sensors.

    We did

    We set three priorities for the hardware: low cost, speed and convenience.

    →
