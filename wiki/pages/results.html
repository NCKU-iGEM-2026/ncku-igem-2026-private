Part 02

Inhibition

Overview

To evaluate the functional effect of our aptamer system, we examined three quorum-sensing-associated phenotypes of Pseudomonas aeruginosa: swarming motility, pyocyanin production, and biofilm formation (Wu et al., 2025). The Cb-2 treatment was prepared from our engineered retron-Cb-2 system, and it is what these assays set out to test. A16-2 and ALS-5 are chemically synthesized reference aptamers from the literature, run alongside it. They are not a head-to-head comparison: the retron-Cb-2 sample is a crude extract, so the amount of aptamer in it is not known the way it is for a synthesized oligonucleotide.

All three are controlled by quorum sensing, so each one reports, from a different angle, on whether the treatment was associated with reduced QS-related phenotypes: swarming is the population-level behavior, pyocyanin the metabolic output closest to the circuit, and biofilm the phenotype that matters most clinically.

Values are normalized to the untreated control run in the same batch. Each treatment was compared with that control (100%) by a one-sample t test; no correction for multiple comparisons was applied.

Swarming assay

Swarming motility was quantified by measuring the relative swarming radius of PAO1 after treatment. Details of the assay conditions are described on the Engineering page.

  • A16-2 chemically synthesized 66.26 ± 18.44% of the untreated control
  • ALS-5 chemically synthesized 77.81 ± 14.36% of the untreated control
  • Cb-2 extract from retron-Cb-2 cells 80.71 ± 5.52% of the untreated control — a 19.3% reduction in swarming radius

All three treatments reduced swarming relative to the untreated control. The retron-Cb-2 sample also showed a relatively narrow spread among replicates.

Pyocyanin inhibition

Pyocyanin production was quantified by chloroform extraction, followed by acidification with 0.2 M HCl and absorbance measurement at 520 nm.

  • A16-2 chemically synthesized 80.16 ± 3.32% of the untreated control
  • ALS-5 chemically synthesized 81.44 ± 6.94% of the untreated control
  • Cb-2 extract from retron-Cb-2 cells 73.72 ± 6.14% of the untreated control — a 26.3% reduction in pyocyanin

The retron-Cb-2 sample reduced pyocyanin production by more than it reduced swarming, and both reference aptamers moved the same way. The reduction in pyocyanin provides an additional functional readout consistent with decreased QS-associated virulence output.

Biofilm Inhibition

Biofilm biomass was quantified by crystal violet staining after 48 h of static incubation and normalized to the untreated control (Wu et al., 2025; O’Toole, 2011; see Protocols).

  • A16-2 chemically synthesized 87.79 ± 8.67% of the control — ~12% reduction (p < 0.05)
  • ALS-5 chemically synthesized 89.02 ± 10.69% of the control — ~11%, not significant (p = 0.053)
  • Cb-2 extract from retron-Cb-2 cells 65.76 ± 10.93% of the control — a 34% reduction (p < 0.0001)

This is the largest reduction observed in the retron-Cb-2 group in any of the three assays, and the most clearly significant of them. Individual measurements ranged from 53% to 89% of the control, so we treat the mean as preliminary and plan to repeat the assay as independent biological replicates under a single set of conditions.

Overall result

Across all three functional assays, the retron-Cb-2 sample was associated with reductions in QS-related phenotypes of P. aeruginosa.

  • Swarming −19.3% swarming radius, with the narrowest spread between replicates
  • Pyocyanin −26.3% pyocyanin production, the metabolite closest to the circuit
  • Biofilm −34% biofilm biomass, the largest effect of the three

Together, these results are consistent with the expected functional effect of Cb-2 on quorum-sensing-associated behaviors.

However, these functional results do not directly confirm that the active ssDNA in our extract was retron-derived Cb-2. Our molecular verification remains inconclusive, as the Engineering page sets out, and the concentration of aptamer in the retron-derived sample was not quantified. Comparisons with the chemically synthesized reference aptamers should therefore not be read as dose-matched comparisons. Further molecular verification and quantification of retron-derived ssDNA will be required.

Even so, the functional picture is a consistent one. Three assays measuring different things — a surface behavior, a secreted metabolite and an adherent structure — all moved in the direction our hypothesis predicts, and did so in the retron-Cb-2 group as well as in the reference aptamers. We take that as meaningful support for the retron-Cb-2 system working as designed, and as the reason the molecular verification is worth completing rather than treating as a formality.
