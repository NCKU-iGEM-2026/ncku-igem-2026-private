Part 02

Inhibition

Overview

To evaluate the functional effect of our aptamer system, we examined three quorum-sensing-associated phenotypes of Pseudomonas aeruginosa: swarming motility, pyocyanin production, and biofilm formation (Wu et al., 2025). Cb-2 is the aptamer our engineered retron produces, and it is what these assays set out to test. ALS-5 and A16-2 are chemically synthesized aptamers taken from the literature; they are included only as benchmarks, to give Cb-2’s effect something to be measured against.

All three are controlled by quorum sensing, so each one reports, from a different angle, on whether the aptamer has taken the signal away: swarming is the population-level behavior, pyocyanin the metabolic output closest to the circuit, and biofilm the phenotype that matters most clinically.

Swarming assay

Swarming motility was quantified by measuring the relative swarming radius of PAO1 after treatment. Detailed optimization of the assay conditions is described on the Engineering page.

  • Cb-2 · ours 80.7 ± 5.52% of the untreated control — a 19.3% reduction in swarming radius
  • A16-2 ~66% reference aptamer
  • ALS-5 ~77% reference aptamer

All three aptamers inhibited swarming. A16-2 and ALS-5 showed greater mean reductions than Cb-2, but both varied widely between replicates, whereas Cb-2 was the most consistent of the three.

Pyocyanin inhibition

Pyocyanin production was quantified by chloroform extraction, followed by acidification with 0.2 M HCl and absorbance measurement at 520 nm.

  • Cb-2 · ours 73.30% of the untreated control — a 26.7% reduction in pyocyanin
  • A16-2 81.90% reference aptamer
  • ALS-5 81.33% reference aptamer

Cb-2 gave the strongest reduction of the three. Where it had led only on consistency in the swarming assay, here it led on the size of the effect as well — and because pyocyanin sits closer to the signal than surface motility does, that is the stronger evidence that the aptamer acts on quorum sensing itself.

Biofilm Inhibition

Biofilm biomass was quantified by crystal violet staining after 48 h of static incubation and normalized to the untreated control (Wu et al., 2025; O’Toole, 2011; see Protocols).

  • Cb-2 · ours 65.76 ± 10.93% of the control — a 34% reduction (p < 0.0001)
  • A16-2 87.79 ± 8.67% ~12% reduction (p < 0.05)
  • ALS-5 89.02 ± 10.69% ~11%, not significant (p = 0.053)

This was the strongest reduction among the three aptamers, and the largest Cb-2 achieved in any assay. It is also the only one that is unambiguous: ALS-5’s reduction did not reach significance. Individual measurements ranged from 53% to 89% of the control, so we treat the mean as preliminary and plan to repeat the assay as independent biological replicates under a single set of conditions.

Overall result

Cb-2 — the aptamer our engineered retron produces — reduced every quorum-sensing phenotype we tested.

  • Swarming −19.3% swarming radius — the most consistent of the three aptamers
  • Pyocyanin −26.7% pyocyanin production — the strongest of the three
  • Biofilm −34% biofilm biomass — the strongest of the three

The three assays agree, and they read the circuit from different distances: a population-level behavior, a metabolite made directly under Rhl and Pqs control, and the structure those behaviors build. A reduction in all three is what we would expect if the aptamer is removing the signal rather than acting on any one pathway downstream of it. Cb-2 was the most effective of the three aptamers on pyocyanin and on biofilm, and the most reproducible on swarming — which matters, because an aptamer that works only sometimes is not one a clinic could use.

None of this is bacterial killing. Growth was not suppressed in these assays, which is the point of an anti-virulence approach: it disarms the population instead of selecting for whatever survives. What the results do not yet show is that the ssDNA doing the work is retron-derived Cb-2 — our gel evidence for that was inconclusive, as the Engineering page sets out. Confirming it needs denaturing urea-PAGE, and the replicate counts here need to be evened out before these numbers are treated as settled.
