CAPTURE

You found our hidden page! Here is our project in a nutshell: instead of fighting Pseudomonas aeruginosa head-on, we listen in on how its cells talk to each other — and then cut the line.

🦠

The problem

P. aeruginosa causes hard-to-treat hospital infections. Its cells coordinate using a chemical signal called AHL.

💡

Detect

Our first system senses the AHL signal and reports it, so the infection becomes visible.

🧬

Inhibit

Our second system captures the same signal, so the bacteria never get the message to act together.
