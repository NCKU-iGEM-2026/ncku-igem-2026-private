    CAPTURE-Screen is a single-cuvette fluorescence reader designed to measure the green fluorescence emitted by our biosensor culture directly at the bench, eliminating the need for a plate reader. We developed two versions of the device: a 3D-printed version and a laser-cut version, each housed in its own enclosure. The following sections describe the features shared by both designs, followed by a detailed overview of each version.

    Background

    Our biosensor reports AHL, the quorum-sensing signal, by making GFP. Reading it takes a small sample, measured every hour, checked against a plate reader, on an instrument cheap enough to rebuild.

    200 µL
    of sfGFP culture per reaction The reporter is superfolder GFP.
    Every hour
    for six hours, at 37 °C A shared plate reader is booked, scheduled and in one building. Either build can sit next to the incubator.
    485 / 510
    nm, the plate-reader benchmark Ours excites at 470 nm, and its geometry, drive current, integration time and dark baseline are ours to state.
    21–65×
    cheaper than an open-source plate reader At most: US$161 for the 3D-printed build and US$54 for the laser-cut one, by our own costing, against under US$3,500 for the plate reader of Szymula and colleagues5. Price only. Anyone with the source files can rebuild ours.
