Ai Wei Li Laser Engraving Studio: the hardware shell

Date: September 10, 2026

While developing the hardware, thought was put into using cost-effective and reusable materials. Therefore, 3D printing and laser cutting were considered as methods of hardware fabrication. Within the period between July and August, a number of materials and designs were used for the hardware case. Nevertheless, most prototypes could not meet the requirements with respect to strength and efficiency.

In order to improve the design, professional consultation was obtained through working with Ai Wei Li (Laser Engraving Studio). The design files were discussed at the studio, and valuable advice was received concerning material choice and fabrication process. Firstly, it should be pointed out that each substance has its own properties and they should be taken into account during the device construction, including light reflection. The importance of the thickness of the material was stressed, especially at the places where different elements are connected, because the incorrect connection may cause light leakage.

Furthermore, assembly and adjustment of the hardware were performed at the studio under the on-site support of the manufacturer.
