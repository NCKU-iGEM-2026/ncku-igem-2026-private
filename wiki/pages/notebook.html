How the season ran

This is our working record for the 2026 season. Each week lists the milestones reached by the three parts of the team — Wet Lab, Dry Lab and Human Practices. Weeks with nothing under them are weeks where no milestone was recorded; we have left the gaps in place rather than closing them, so the record reads as it happened.
