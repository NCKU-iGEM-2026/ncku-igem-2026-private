The retron system mainly consists of three parts — two strands of non-coding RNA, msd and msr, and a reverse transcriptase (RT), which is able to reverse transcribe the RNA sequence of the msd to ssDNA. The msr is a conservative sequence, which forms a stem structure for the reverse transcriptase to bind to the RNA. In addition, the msd is the sequence that the RT will reverse transcribe.

By changing the msd sequence, this part could be used as the system to produce different kinds of ssDNA aptamers.
