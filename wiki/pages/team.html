Hello everyone! I’m Hank, the captain of this year’s NCKU iGEM team. I hail from Xihu (the “Stream Lake,” definitely not the famous “West Lake”).

My greatest passions in life? LEGO and Medicine. My daily grind? A glorious mix of parasites and chemistry.

I’ve been completely overwhelmed with work this year, held hostage by my busy schedule. You’ll usually find me spending all my time keeping company with endless research papers. Between my daily chores and research, I have way too much on my plate. If you need me, feel free to call my cell—though just a heads-up, it’s always on mute.

Welcome to the iGEM family! Let’s create some unforgettable memories of our youth together!
