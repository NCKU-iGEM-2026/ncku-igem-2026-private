Attributions

Our deepest gratitude goes to everyone who has accompanied and supported us throughout this journey. Your wisdom, mentorship, and unwavering encouragement have shaped us into a stronger and more resilient team. Every piece of guidance, every word of encouragement, and every helping hand has brought us one step further. We could never have reached this point without you, and for that, we are sincerely and eternally grateful.

Use of generative AI

We used an AI coding assistant while building this wiki, and we are disclosing that here. Everything published under our name remains our responsibility: each of us has read and understood the work described below before it was committed.

Where we used it

  • Website code. Implementing the wiki's layout and styling, the SDG board game on our Board Game page, its cross-device multiplayer, and the interactive Taiwan map on our Education page.
  • Fixing what was broken. Finding and correcting layout faults, including a navigation menu that could not be opened on phones, and map pins that could not be clicked.
  • Testing. Writing automated browser tests that check the pages and the board game across screen sizes and devices.
  • Transcription. Converting our own card database and reference list into the format the game code reads.
