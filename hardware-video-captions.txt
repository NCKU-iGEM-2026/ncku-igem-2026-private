  SHOT 01   0:00   12 s   SETUP
  The answer is light. Reading it is the problem.

  EN  Our cell-free reaction answers an AHL signal by making sfGFP. Reading that answer normally means a benchtop plate reader.

  ZH  我們的無細胞反應接收到 AHL 訊號後，會做出 sfGFP。而要讀出這個答案，平常得靠一台桌上型盤式讀取儀。


  SHOT 02   0:12   14 s   SETUP
  Why build a reader at all

  EN  A shared reader is booked, scheduled, and in someone else's building. So we built our own. Cheap enough to sit next to the incubator, and open enough that anyone can rebuild it.

  ZH  共用的讀取儀要排隊、要預約，而且只待在某一棟樓裡。所以我們自己做了一台——便宜到可以放在培養箱旁邊，而且開放到任何人都能照著重建。


  SHOT 03   0:26   12 s   SETUP
  Title — CAPTURE-Screen

  EN  This is CAPTURE-Screen: a research-use, single-cuvette, ninety-degree fluorescence reader. Here is how it works.

  ZH  這是 CAPTURE-Screen：一台研究用途、單一比色管、90 度螢光讀取儀。以下是它的原理。


  SHOT 04   0:38   22 s   PRINCIPLE
  Excitation — one blue LED at 470 nm

  EN  It starts with one blue LED, a Cree part, nominal four hundred and seventy nanometres. sfGFP takes in blue light and gives back green. Our benchmark plate reader excites at four eighty-five. Four seventy is deliberately different, and that difference is something we will have to characterise, not assume away.

  ZH  一切從一顆藍光 LED 開始——Cree 的零件，標稱 470 奈米。sfGFP 吸收藍光、放出綠光。我們對照用的盤式讀取儀是用 485 奈米激發；470 是刻意不一樣的，這個差異我們得去量出來，而不是假裝它不存在。


  SHOT 05   1:00   20 s   PRINCIPLE
  Look across the beam, not down it

  EN  Here is the central idea. The excitation beam is overwhelmingly brighter than the fluorescence we are trying to see. So the detector does not look down the beam. It looks across it, at ninety degrees. On that axis it sees the sample, and almost none of the source.

  ZH  這是最核心的想法。激發光遠遠比我們想看的螢光亮得多。所以偵測器不從光路正後方看，而是從側邊、90 度的方向看。在那個軸上，它看到的是樣品，而幾乎看不到光源。


  SHOT 06   1:20   22 s   PRINCIPLE
  What happens to the light we don't want

  EN  Before the sample, two aperture stops trim the beam. After it, whatever the sample does not absorb runs into an angled wedge trap, slanted so that light is deflected rather than returned. The far side of the axis belongs to that trap, which is what makes this a fluorescence geometry, not a transmission one.

  ZH  在樣品之前，兩片光闌把光束修窄。在樣品之後，沒有被吸收的光會進入一個斜角楔形的吸光阱；斜面讓光被偏折掉，而不是反射回來。光軸的另一側屬於這個吸光阱——這正是為什麼它是一台螢光儀器，而不是一台穿透式儀器。


  SHOT 07   1:42   18 s   PRINCIPLE
  The sample — 2 mm across, 10 mm along

  EN  The sample sits in an Eppendorf UVette, in one fixed orientation we call ORIENT-A. The excitation crosses the short two-millimetre path. The detector looks along the ten-millimetre one. Two hundred microlitres.

  ZH  樣品放在 Eppendorf UVette 裡，用一個固定方向，我們叫它 ORIENT-A。激發光穿過 2 毫米的短光程，偵測器則沿著 10 毫米的長光程看過去。每次 200 微升。


  SHOT 08   2:00   22 s   PRINCIPLE
  The detector — channels, not a single number

  EN  The detector is an AS7341, a spectral sensor with separate colour channels, not a single photodiode. That lets us watch the excitation band and the emission band apart, and measure the leakage between them. The slot in front of it holds the emission filter, and is currently open.

  ZH  偵測器是 AS7341——一顆有多個獨立顏色通道的光譜感測器，而不是單一光電二極體。這讓我們可以分開看激發波段和放射波段，並量出兩者之間的洩漏。它前面那個位置是給放射濾光片的，目前是空的。


  SHOT 09   2:22   22 s   OPERATION
  How one reading runs

  EN  A reading is a timed window. The LED comes on, a hundred milliseconds to settle, then four hundred and fifty-two to read every channel. About five hundred and fifty milliseconds in all, inside a one-second hard timeout. That window repeats once an hour, for six hours at thirty-seven degrees.

  ZH  一次讀值就是一個有時限的視窗。LED 亮起，先穩定 100 毫秒，再用 452 毫秒讀完所有通道——全部大約 550 毫秒，落在 1 秒的硬性逾時之內。這個視窗每小時重複一次，在 37 度下連續六小時。


  SHOT 10   2:44   16 s   OPERATION
  What the detector sees

  EN  This is what the detector reads. With a scatterer in the sample seat, it sees the excitation. Lid closed and the room lit, every channel reads zero. LED on, nothing in the seat, still zero.

  ZH  這是偵測器實際讀到的東西。在樣品座放一個散射體，它看得到激發光。蓋上、開著室內燈，每個通道都讀到 0。LED 開著、樣品座是空的，還是 0。


  SHOT 11   3:00   12 s   CLAIM

  ZH  所以，精確地說：CAPTURE-Screen 讀的是相對的生物感測器螢光強度，並標記光學干擾。

  Our cell-free reaction answers an AHL signal by making sfGFP. Reading that answer normally means a benchtop plate reader.

The far side of the axis belongs to that trap, which is what makes this a fluorescence geometry, not a transmission one.
